### About Me

I'm Denny, 23 — a Data Scientist and ML Engineer. I build machine-learning and automation
pipelines: from data collection and analysis to models and services that quietly do the work
for someone every day.

- **Now** — procurement automation for a client's company, and content-generation automation
  for a realistic AI persona of my own.
- **Studying** — Data Science at **School 21**, where I work through pandas, SQL, data
  visualization and classic ML, and publish every completed project.
- **Generative AI** — I train LoRA models and build custom ComfyUI workflows.
- **Open source** — [**grabogram**](https://github.com/DennyBeus/grabogram): a self-hosted Telegram
  channel parser you drive entirely from a Telegram bot. Docker + `aiogram` + `kurigram`,
  proxy support, `txt`/`json` export straight into your chat — your data never leaves your server.
